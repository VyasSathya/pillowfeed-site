How to hide videos on iPhone with a password

Apple's pages describe no password for a single video. What the iPhone has is locks in front of the place the video sits, and each of them opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode.

1. The Hidden album

  • Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to Utilities, then Hidden.
  • The album is locked by default on iOS 16 and later, and opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The lock is the Use Face ID setting in Settings, then Apps, then Photos. Apple says that if you turn it off, the Hidden album is unlocked by default.

The video is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it. Anyone who can pass Face ID or Touch ID, or who knows your passcode, can open the album.

2. Lock the Photos app

Touch and hold the Photos icon on the Home Screen and tap Require Face ID. The whole app, library and Hidden album, then needs Face ID or the passcode. With Stolen Device Protection on, away from familiar places it needs Face ID or Touch ID, with no passcode option.

3. An app with its own lock

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, so it has no password of its own. It locks again after 5 minutes away, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your videos. Import from Photos or Files, then delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted if you want them gone from the library.

Why not a locked note

Notes can use a password separate from your passcode, but Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked. So a note does not protect a video.

Except in that Stolen Device Protection case, someone who knows your iPhone passcode can open all three, so keep it private.

Common questions

How do I hide a video on iPhone with a password?

Apple's pages describe no password for one video. Put it in the Hidden album, lock the Photos app with Face ID, or keep it in a separate app with its own lock. The Hidden album and a locked app both open with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

How do I password protect a video on iPhone?

Apple's pages describe no password of its own for a video. A note with a video attached cannot be locked, so Notes does not work. The Hidden album and a locked app both open with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

How do I put a video in Hidden on iPhone?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to Utilities, then Hidden, which opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
