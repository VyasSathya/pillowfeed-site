How to hide videos on iPhone with a password

The iPhone has no password for a single video. What it has is locks in front of the place the video sits, and every one of them opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode.

1. The Hidden album

  • Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to Utilities, then Hidden.
  • The album asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. The setting is in Settings, then Apps, then Photos, then Use Face ID.

The video stays in your library, and anyone who knows your passcode can open the album.

2. Lock the Photos app

On iOS 18 and later, touch and hold the Photos icon on the Home Screen and tap Require Face ID. The whole app, library and Hidden album, then needs Face ID or the passcode.

3. An app with its own lock

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, so it has no password of its own. It locks again after 5 minutes away, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your videos. Import from Photos or Files, then delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted if you want them gone from the library.

Why not a locked note

Notes can use a password separate from your passcode, but Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked. So a note does not protect a video.

Whichever you pick, the iPhone passcode is the real lock, so keep it private.

Common questions

How do I hide a video on iPhone with a password?

The iPhone has no password for one video. Put it in the Hidden album, lock the Photos app with Face ID on iOS 18 or later, or keep it in a separate app with its own lock. All of them open with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

How do I password protect a video on iPhone?

You cannot protect a video with a password of its own. A note with a video attached cannot be locked, so Notes does not work. The Hidden album and a locked app use Face ID or your passcode.

How do I put a video in Hidden on iPhone?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to Utilities, then Hidden, which opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
