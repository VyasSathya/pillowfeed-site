How to hide videos on iPhone with a password

The iPhone has no password for a single video. What you can do is put a lock in front of the place the video sits. There are three ways, and they use different locks.

1. A locked note, with its own password

  • In Notes, make a new note and add the video from Photos.
  • Lock the note from its menu. You can use your iPhone passcode, or set a separate Notes password that only opens locked notes. Face ID or Touch ID opens it once set up.
  • Delete the original from Photos, then empty Recently Deleted, or it stays visible there.

This is the only way here to get a password that is not your phone passcode.

2. The Hidden album, behind Face ID or the passcode

  • Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to Utilities, then Hidden.
  • The album asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. The setting is in Settings, then Apps, then Photos, then Use Face ID.

There is no separate password for it. Anyone who knows your passcode can open it, and the video stays in your library.

3. An app with its own lock

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, so it has no password of its own. It locks again after 5 minutes away, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your videos. Import from Photos or Files, then delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted if you want them gone from the library.

If you want a password that is different from your passcode, use a locked note. For anything else, the iPhone passcode is the real lock, so keep it private.
