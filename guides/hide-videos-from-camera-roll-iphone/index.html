How to hide videos from your camera roll on iPhone

The iPhone has no gallery and no secret folder. The camera roll is the Photos app, and there are two ways to take a video out of what people see when they scroll it.

1. The Hidden album

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves the camera roll and sits in the Hidden album, behind Face ID or Touch ID. The video stays in your library.

2. A separate app

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. Import from Photos, then delete the originals.

Check Recently Deleted

A video you delete from Photos goes to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, and it can still be opened there. Empty it, or it is not gone.

Common questions

How do I hide a video in my camera roll?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves the camera roll and goes to the Hidden album.

How do I hide videos in the gallery on my iPhone?

The iPhone has no gallery app. Your photos and videos are in Photos. Use the Hidden album or a separate app.

Where can I hide a video in iOS secretly?

The Hidden album under Utilities is the built-in place. A separate app with its own lock is the other.
