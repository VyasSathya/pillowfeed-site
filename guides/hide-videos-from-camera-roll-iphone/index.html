How to hide videos from your camera roll on iPhone

On iPhone, your photos and videos are in the Photos app, and its built-in hiding place is the Hidden album. There are two ways to take a video out of what people see when they scroll your Library.

1. The Hidden album

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves your Library and sits in the Hidden album, behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. It is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it.

2. A separate app

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. Import from Photos, then delete the originals.

Check Recently Deleted

A video you delete from Photos goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, and it can still be recovered there. Delete it from Recently Deleted too, or it stays there for 30 days before it is removed.

Common questions

How do I hide a video in my camera roll?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves your Library and goes to the Hidden album.

How do I hide videos in the gallery on my iPhone?

On iPhone, your photos and videos are in the Photos app. Use the Hidden album or a separate app.

Where can I hide a video in iOS secretly?

The Hidden album under Utilities is the built-in place. A separate app with its own lock is the other.
