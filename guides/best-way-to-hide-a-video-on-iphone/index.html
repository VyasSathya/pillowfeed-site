The best way to hide a video on iPhone, by who you are hiding it from

There is no single best way. Pick by who is looking, and be honest about what each one can stop.

Someone scrolling your camera roll

Use the Hidden album. Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, tap Hide. Turn on Use Face ID in Settings, then Apps, then Photos.

Someone who borrows your phone

On iOS 18 and later, touch and hold the Photos icon and choose Require Face ID. The whole app is then locked, so the library and the Hidden album both need Face ID or the passcode.

You want the video out of your library

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. Import from Photos, then delete the originals and empty Recently Deleted.

Someone who knows your passcode

The passcode opens every lock above. The one exception is a locked note with its own Notes password, different from your passcode; delete the original from Photos and Recently Deleted after adding it. Keep your passcode private, and change it if it has been seen.

Common questions

What is the best way to hide a video on iPhone?

It depends on who you are hiding it from. The Hidden album suits a casual scroll, a locked app suits a borrowed phone, and a separate app keeps it out of your camera roll. None of them stops someone who knows your passcode.

Is there a secret mode on iPhone?

There is no secret mode. The closest built-in tools are the Hidden album, locked notes, and requiring Face ID to open an app.

How do I hide a video in my camera roll?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to the Hidden album.
