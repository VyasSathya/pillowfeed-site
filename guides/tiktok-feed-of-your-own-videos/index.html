A TikTok-style feed of your own videos on iPhone

The Photos app shows a grid. If you want to swipe through your own clips one after another, full screen, the way TikTok plays, you need an app built for it.

What Photos can and cannot do

Photos plays one item at a time, and you swipe sideways through photos and videos mixed together in date order. Memories mixes in stills and music you did not pick. There is no endless vertical feed of only your videos.

Pillowfeed

Pillowfeed turns the photos and videos you add into a private vertical feed:

  • Swipe up for the next one, newest first or shuffled
  • Heart the ones you come back to, mark the best as Super, and play just those
  • Filter to videos with sound, or play one album as its own feed
  • Bring media in from Photos, Files, the share sheet, a pasted link or the built-in browser

The app opens with Face ID and locks again after 5 minutes away. There is no account and no ads. With Pillowfeed Pro your collection can move into your own iCloud, so a large feed does not fill the phone. The first 200 items are free.

To see how a feed of your own clips feels before installing anything, try it in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
