How to hide downloaded videos on iPhone

A video you save from the web lands in Photos or in the Files app, and each has a different way to hide it. There is also a third place that gives it away: Safari's own download list.

If it is in Photos

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to the Hidden album under Utilities, which opens with Face ID or Touch ID.

If it is in Files

Safari saves downloads to the Downloads folder in the Files app. Files cannot hide a file. You can lock the whole app on iOS 18 and later: touch and hold the Files icon and choose Require Face ID.

Clear Safari's download list

Safari keeps a list of what you downloaded, even after you move the file. Open the Downloads button in the address bar menu and choose Clear. This clears the list only; the file stays where it is.

Keep it out of both places

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. Common questions

How do I hide a saved video on iPhone?

If it is in Photos, open it, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. If it is in Files, the Files app cannot hide it, so lock the app with Face ID on iOS 18 or later, or move the video into a separate app.

How do I hide downloaded files on iPhone?

The Files app has no hide option. Lock the Files app with Require Face ID, or keep the file in an app that has its own lock.

How do I clear the download list in Safari?

In Safari, tap the Downloads button in the address bar menu and choose Clear. This removes the list, not the files.
