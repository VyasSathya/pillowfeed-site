How to hide downloaded videos on iPhone

A video you download in Safari goes to a Downloads folder in the Files app; if you saved it to Photos, it is in your library. Each has a different way to hide it. There is also a third place that gives it away: Safari's own download list.

If it is in Photos

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It moves to the Hidden album under Utilities, which opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

If it is in Files

Safari saves downloads to a Downloads folder in iCloud Drive or On My iPhone, or to another place you choose in Settings, Apps, Safari, Downloads. Apple's Files guide lists copy, move, share and delete for a file, but no hide. You can lock the whole app: touch and hold the Files icon and choose Require Face ID.

Safari's download list

After a download, a Downloads button appears next to Safari's search field and lists your downloads.

Keep it out of both places

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. You can save the video straight into it from the built-in browser or a pasted link, so it never lands in Photos or Files.

Common questions

How do I hide a saved video on iPhone?

If it is in Photos, open it, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. If it is in Files, Apple's Files guide lists no hide option, so lock the app with Face ID, or move the video into a separate app.

How do I hide downloaded files on iPhone?

Apple's Files guide lists no hide option for a file. Lock the Files app with Require Face ID, or keep the file in an app that has its own lock.

How do I clear the download list in Safari?

After a download, a Downloads button appears next to Safari's search field and lists your downloads. Apple's page does not describe clearing it.
