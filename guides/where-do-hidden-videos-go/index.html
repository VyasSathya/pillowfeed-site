When you hide a video on iPhone, where does it go?

It goes to the Hidden album, under Utilities in Photos. The video is not deleted and not moved anywhere else: it is only taken out of the places people normally scroll.

What changes when you hide it

  • It stops showing in your library views and in your other albums.
  • It shows only in the Hidden album, which asks for Face ID or Touch ID.

What stays the same

  • It is the same file, in the same library.
  • If iCloud Photos is on, it still syncs to your other devices on your Apple Account, and it is hidden there too.
  • Anyone who can pass Face ID or Touch ID on your phone can open the Hidden album.

How to get it back

Open Photos, go to Utilities, tap Hidden, open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Unhide.

If you want it somewhere other than your library

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

Common questions

Where do hidden videos go on iPhone?

To the Hidden album, under Utilities in Photos. The video stays in your library and is only removed from the main library views and from other albums.

How do I recover my hidden videos?

Open Photos, go to Utilities, tap Hidden, open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Unhide. It returns to your library.

Can you hide videos on an iPhone?

Yes. Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide.
