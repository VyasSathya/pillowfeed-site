When you hide a video on iPhone, where does it go?

It goes to the Hidden album, under Utilities in Photos. It is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it.

What changes when you hide it

  • It stops showing in your library views and in your other albums.
  • It shows only in the Hidden album, which asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

What stays the same

  • It is not deleted: when you unhide it, it reappears in your photo library.
  • If iCloud Photos is on, it still syncs to your other devices on your Apple Account, and it is hidden there too.
  • Anyone who can pass Face ID or Touch ID on your phone, or who knows your passcode, can open the Hidden album.

How to get it back

Open Photos, tap Collections, tap Hidden under Utilities, tap View Album and unlock, open the video, tap the More button, and tap Unhide.

If you want it somewhere other than your library

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

Open Photos, tap Collections, tap Hidden under Utilities, tap View Album and unlock, open the video, tap the More button, and tap Unhide. It returns to your library.

Yes. Open the video in Photos, tap the More button, and tap Hide.
