How to hide videos on iPhone

iPhone has a built-in way to hide videos. It is enough for some people. If you want private videos out of your library completely, use a separate locked app.

The Hidden album in Photos

  • Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu and tap Hide.
  • It moves to Utilities, then Hidden, which needs Face ID to open.

The video is still in your Photos library. It still syncs to iCloud Photos and every device signed in to your Apple Account, and it is still counted in your library's storage.

A separate locked feed

Pillowfeed keeps private photos and videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll:

  • Opens with Face ID or your passcode, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your media

There is no account, and the developer runs no servers, so your media never passes through the developer.

Common questions

How do I hide videos on iPhone?

In Photos, open the video, tap the three-dot menu and tap Hide; it moves to Utilities > Hidden, which needs Face ID. It stays in your Photos library and iCloud Photos.
