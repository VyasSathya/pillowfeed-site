Hidden album not showing on iPhone: how to get it back

If the Hidden album has vanished from Photos, a setting is almost always the reason, and it takes a few seconds to switch back on.

Turn the album back on

  • Open Settings, then Apps, then Photos.
  • Turn on Show Hidden Album.
  • Open Photos, go to Albums or Collections, scroll to Utilities, and tap Hidden.

With the setting off, the album is not listed at all, even though everything you hid is still there.

It asks for Face ID or the passcode

That is normal. The album is locked by default on iOS 16 and later, and you open it with Face ID or Touch ID.

Your hidden items are not in the main library

A hidden video or photo is taken out of the library views and out of other albums on purpose. It appears only in the Hidden album.

Still empty?

If the album shows but is empty, see our guide on a missing Hidden album: the usual causes are a deleted item, a different Apple Account, or iCloud Photos still syncing.

Common questions

Why isn't my Hidden album showing up on iPhone?

Most often the Show Hidden Album setting is off. Open Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn on Show Hidden Album. The album then appears under Utilities.

How do I find the Hidden album on iPhone?

Open Photos, go to Albums or Collections, scroll to Utilities, and tap Hidden. It asks for Face ID or Touch ID.

Why can't I see my hidden photos in the main library?

That is how hiding works. A hidden photo or video is removed from the library views and other albums and only shows in the Hidden album.
