Where do downloaded videos go on iPhone?

A file you download from the internet goes to the Downloads folder in the Files app. A video you save to Photos from another app, Safari included, goes to the Recently Saved collection in Photos.

Find it in Files

  • Open the Files app. If it is not on your Home Screen, swipe down from the center of the Home Screen and search for Files.
  • Tap Browse.
  • Tap the Downloads folder. If you don't see it on the Browse screen, tap iCloud Drive first.

To see files you opened recently, tap Recents.

Right after a Safari download

Tap the Downloads button next to the search field in Safari, then tap Downloads. Apple says this is also where you check the status of a file that is still downloading.

Change where Safari saves

Go to Settings, tap Apps, then Safari, then Downloads. iCloud Drive saves to the Downloads folder in iCloud Drive, which you can open on any iPhone, iPad or Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. On My iPhone saves to the Downloads folder on your iPhone. Other saves to a different folder you pick.

Find it in Photos

Open Photos, tap Collections, then tap Recently Saved below Pinned. It is also listed below Utilities. For what you saved today, tap Collections, then Saved Today below Recent Days.

Saved in the Reddit app

Reddit's Save button is for posts you want to revisit later. To see it, go to your profile page, tap the menu with three lines near the top right, then tap Saved.

Move it somewhere private

In Files, touch and hold a file to move it or share it.

Keep it in Pillowfeed

Pillowfeed is an iPhone app with its own feed for the videos you keep, apart from your camera roll. Once the video is in Files or Photos, import it from there, or touch and hold it in Files, tap Share and pick Pillowfeed. Shortcuts can pass it on too, with the Add to Pillowfeed action. Pillowfeed opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher instead of your videos. Pillowfeed Free holds 200 photos and videos.

Common questions

Where do downloaded videos from Reddit go on iPhone?

A video you download in Safari goes to the Downloads folder in the Files app, in iCloud Drive or on your iPhone, as set in Settings, Apps, Safari, Downloads. Reddit's own Save button keeps the post in your Saved list inside Reddit.

When you save a video on Reddit, where does it go?

To your Saved list in Reddit. In the Reddit app, go to your profile page, tap the menu with three lines near the top right, then tap Saved.

Why can't I find my downloaded video?

In Files, tap Browse and look for Downloads; if it is not on the Browse screen, tap iCloud Drive. Apple also says to open Safari and tap the Downloads button to check the status of a file that is still downloading.
