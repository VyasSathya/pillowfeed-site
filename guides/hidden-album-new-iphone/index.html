Does the Hidden album transfer to a new iPhone?

Yes. Hidden photos and videos move with the rest of your library and stay hidden. What does not move on its own is anything an app keeps for itself, and that includes some privacy apps. Here is what comes across and what needs setting up again.

Photos: the Hidden album

  • iCloud Photos on: your library, hidden items included, syncs to your Apple Account. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone and the Hidden album is there, locked with Face ID or Touch ID.
  • iCloud Photos off: a device backup or the direct iPhone-to-iPhone transfer during setup carries the library, and the hidden items stay hidden.
  • Set up Face ID on the new iPhone so the Hidden album can open.

Locked notes

Notes stored in iCloud sync to the new iPhone. A locked note still needs the password or passcode that locks it. To use your device passcode for locked notes, iCloud Keychain must be on. Keep the Notes password written down: Apple cannot recover it.

Files

Files in iCloud Drive sync. Files saved under On My iPhone come across with a backup or the direct transfer. If you locked the Files or Photos app with Face ID, check that Require Face ID is still on for each.

Apps that keep their own collection

Each app decides. Pillowfeed is a plain case: its own screen says that without iCloud, your collection is only on that iPhone, iPhone backups do not include it, and moving to a new iPhone leaves it behind. To bring it with you:

  • On the old iPhone, turn on iCloud in Pillowfeed (Pro) under Storage and backup, and let the backup finish.
  • On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple Account and install Pillowfeed.
  • On the first screen, tap Restore your collection. It is also under Storage and backup.
  • Tap Restore on the Pro screen to bring back your purchase.

Do this before you erase or trade in the old iPhone.

A checklist for the day you switch

  • iCloud Photos on and finished syncing, or a fresh backup.
  • The Notes password written down.
  • Any app with its own collection backed up inside that app.
  • Only then, erase the old iPhone.

Common questions

Does the Hidden album transfer to a new iPhone?

Yes. With iCloud Photos on, hidden items sync to your Apple Account and stay hidden on the new iPhone. A backup restore or a direct iPhone-to-iPhone transfer also brings them across.

How do I transfer hidden photos from one iPhone to another?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Photos on, or restore a backup, or use the direct transfer while setting up the new iPhone. Then open Photos, Utilities, Hidden on the new iPhone.

Will my hidden photos transfer to iCloud?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is on. They sync to iCloud and stay hidden on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

How do I move photos out of the Hidden album?

Open the Hidden album, open the photo or video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Unhide. It returns to your library.
