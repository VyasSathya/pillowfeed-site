How to hide videos on iPhone in the Files app

The Files app cannot hide a video, and it cannot lock one folder. What it can do is keep a video out of your Photos library, and on iOS 18 and later you can lock the whole Files app behind Face ID. Together that works, with limits.

Step by step

  • In Photos, open the video, tap Share, and choose Save to Files. Pick a folder under On My iPhone.
  • Delete the video from Photos, then empty Recently Deleted. Until you do, it is in both places.
  • Press and hold the Files icon on the Home Screen and choose Require Face ID. Opening Files now needs Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

What stays visible

  • The folder and the file name are visible to anyone who gets into Files. Name them with that in mind.
  • Recents lists files you opened lately, so the video can show up there until you open other files.
  • There is no password for one folder. The lock is on the whole app, and the iPhone passcode opens it.

Hiding the app too

On iOS 18 and later, press and hold the Files icon and choose Hide and Require Face ID, instead of plain Require Face ID, to move the app out of sight into a locked Hidden folder in the App Library. This hides the app, not the video: the video is still in Files.

Common questions

Is there a way to hide a video on iPhone?

Yes: the Hidden album in Photos, a folder in the Files app with the Files app locked, or a separate app with its own lock. The Files app itself has no hide option.

How do I put videos in a hidden folder on iPhone?

The Files app has no hidden folders. You can save the video to a folder in Files, then lock the whole Files app with Require Face ID on iOS 18 or later. The Hidden album in Photos is the built-in hidden place.

How do I hide files on my iPhone?

The Files app cannot hide files. Lock the app with Face ID, hide the app itself, or move the files into an app that has its own lock.
