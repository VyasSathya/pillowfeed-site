Can the iPhone Hidden album be Face ID only?

Apple's pages describe no Face ID only option. Apple says the Hidden album is locked by default on iOS 16 and later, and that you unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. The one setting Apple lists turns the lock on or off: Settings, Apps, Photos, Use Face ID. Turning it off unlocks the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums.

What Apple says

  • "Your Hidden album is locked by default and requires authentication to unlock in Photos," on iOS 16 and later.
  • To open it: "Tap View Album, then use Face ID or Touch ID to unlock your Hidden album."
  • "In iOS 16, iPadOS 16.1, or later, Face ID or Touch ID is required to view the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums by default."
  • "If you turn off Use Face ID, the Hidden album will also be unlocked by default."
  • Apple's pages describe no password of its own for the album. Apple's iPhone User Guide says: "You can unlock these collections using Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode."

What you can change

  • Turn the lock off or back on. Open Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn Use Face ID off or on. Off unlocks the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums.
  • Take it out of sight. Open Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn off Show Hidden Album. The album is no longer listed in Photos, and the hidden items stay hidden.
  • Lock the whole Photos app. Touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID. Apple says a locked app opens with "Face ID (or Touch ID or a passcode)", so this is not Face ID only either.

What stays the same

The hidden video is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it. If you use iCloud Photos, the photos you hide on one device are hidden on your other devices too.

A separate locked place for videos

Pillowfeed is an app that keeps videos apart from Photos. It checks your identity with Face ID and falls back to the iPhone passcode, so it is not Face ID only. What differs is where the videos live: inside the app, not in your camera roll.

  • It locks again after 5 minutes away from the app, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your videos.
  • Import from Photos or Files, then delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted if you want them out of the library.
  • No account, and the developer runs no servers.
