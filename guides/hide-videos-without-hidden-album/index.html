How to hide videos on iPhone without the Hidden album

The Hidden album is the obvious answer, and it has limits: it stays in your library, syncs to iCloud Photos, and opens with your passcode. Here are four other ways, and what each one does.

Notes, Files and a separate app all copy the video. The original stays in Photos until you delete it there and empty Recently Deleted.

1. A locked note

  • In Notes, make a new note and add the video from Photos.
  • Lock the note from its menu. You can use your iPhone passcode or a separate Notes password, and Face ID or Touch ID opens it once set up.

Good for a few clips. Large videos make a heavy note.

2. A folder in Files, with the Files app locked

  • Save the video into a folder in the Files app.
  • Files has no lock for single folders. On iOS 18 and later you can press and hold the Files icon on the Home Screen and choose Require Face ID, which locks the whole app.

3. Lock the Photos app itself

  • On iOS 18 and later, press and hold the Photos icon and choose Require Face ID.
  • This hides everything in Photos from someone who borrows your phone. It does not separate the videos from the rest of your library, and the passcode still opens it.

4. A separate app with its own lock

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. Videos play as a full-screen feed you swipe through, and you can add them from Photos, Files, a pasted link or the built-in browser. The first 200 items are free, and Pro keeps the collection in your own iCloud.

If someone knows your passcode, they can open any of these four. The passcode is the real lock.

Common questions

How do I hide videos on iPhone without the Hidden album?

Put the video in a locked note, in a folder in the Files app with the Files app locked, lock the Photos app with Face ID on iOS 18 or later, or keep the video in a separate app with its own lock. The original stays in Photos until you delete it.

Does hiding a video in Notes remove it from Photos?

No. Adding a video to a note makes a copy. The original stays in Photos until you delete it and empty Recently Deleted.
