How to hide videos on iPhone without the Hidden album

The Hidden album is the obvious answer, and it has limits: the video is not deleted, and if you use iCloud Photos it is hidden on your other devices too. Here are three other ways, and what each one does.

After you save a video to Files or import it into a separate app, delete the original from Photos, then from Recently Deleted, or Photos keeps it there for 30 days. Notes is not an option: Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked.

1. A folder in Files, with the Files app locked

  • Save the video into a folder in the Files app.
  • Apple's pages describe no lock for a single folder in Files. You can press and hold the Files icon on the Home Screen and choose Require Face ID, which locks the whole app.

2. Lock the Photos app itself

  • Press and hold the Photos icon and choose Require Face ID.
  • This hides everything in Photos from someone who borrows your phone. The passcode still opens it, unless Stolen Device Protection is on and you are away from a familiar place.

3. A separate app with its own lock

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. Videos play as a full-screen feed you swipe through, and you can add them from Photos, Files, a pasted link or the built-in browser. The first 200 items are free, and Pro keeps the collection in your own iCloud.

Except when Stolen Device Protection asks for Face ID or Touch ID, someone who knows your passcode can open any of these three.
