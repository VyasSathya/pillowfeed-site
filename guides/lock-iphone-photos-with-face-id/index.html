Can you lock the Photos app with Face ID on iPhone?

Yes. The Hidden album has been locked by default since iOS 16, and you can lock the whole Photos app as well.

Lock the whole Photos app

  • Touch and hold the Photos icon on the Home Screen.
  • Tap Require Face ID (or Touch ID or Passcode), and confirm.

Opening Photos now asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. There is no separate password for the app.

Lock only the Hidden album

On iOS 16 and later the Hidden album is locked by default, and you open it with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. No setting is needed. The Recently Deleted album is locked the same way. To unlock both by default, turn off Use Face ID in Settings, then Apps, then Photos.

What it does not do

  • Someone who knows your iPhone passcode can open Photos.
  • Photos locks again only after you quit it.

If you want videos out of Photos

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

Common questions

Can I put Face ID on my Photos?

Yes. Touch and hold the Photos icon on the Home Screen and tap Require Face ID.

Can I put a lock on Photos on my iPhone?

Yes. Require Face ID locks the whole Photos app. The Hidden album has had its own lock since iOS 16.

Can you put a password lock on Photos?

There is no separate password for Photos. The lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode.
