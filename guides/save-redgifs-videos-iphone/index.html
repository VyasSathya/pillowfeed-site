How to save RedGIFs videos on iPhone

RedGIFs' help center describes saving only your own uploads: verified users can download them from the Manage page, and RedGIFs Studio lists "Download Your Own Content" too. For anything else, its Terms of Service say you may not download RedGIFs content "without the prior written consent of RedGIFs or, if applicable, the relevant licensor."

Your own uploads

  • Go to your Manage page on RedGIFs.
  • Select the content you want to download.
  • Tap the three-dot button under Actions, then choose Download. RedGIFs says the file starts saving to your device right away.

RedGIFs says the Download option is only there for verified users, on their own uploads. If you are not verified yet, you finish verification first.

Clips other people posted

RedGIFs' help pages give no steps for saving someone else's clip, and its terms ask for written consent from RedGIFs or the licensor first. Save only what you have the right to keep.

RedGIFs on iPhone

The RedGIFs app for iPhone is added from Safari: open Safari, go to redgifs.com, tap the share icon, then tap Add to Home Screen.

Where the file goes

Safari saves downloads to a Downloads folder in iCloud Drive or on your iPhone. You pick which in Settings: tap Apps, then Safari, then Downloads. To find the file, open the Files app, tap Browse, then tap Downloads. If you don't see the Downloads folder on the Browse screen, tap iCloud Drive.

Videos you save to Photos from another app, Safari included, are in Photos: tap Collections, then tap Recently Saved below Pinned.

Keep it in Pillowfeed

Pillowfeed is an iPhone app with its own feed for the videos you keep, apart from your camera roll. Once the video is in Files or Photos, import it from there, or touch and hold it in Files, tap Share and pick Pillowfeed. Shortcuts can pass it on too, with the Add to Pillowfeed action. Pillowfeed opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher instead of your videos. Pillowfeed Free holds 200 photos and videos.

Common questions

Can you save RedGIFs videos?

RedGIFs lets verified users download their own uploads from the Manage page. Its Terms of Service say any other download needs the prior written consent of RedGIFs or the relevant licensor.

Can you save GIFs to an iPhone camera roll?

Apple's guide says that, depending on the app, you tap the Save button next to the image, or touch and hold the photo or video and then tap the Save button. Items you save from other apps, Safari included, show up in the Recently Saved collection in Photos.

How do I get sound on RedGIFs clips in the Reddit app?

RedGIFs says Reddit's official apps have historically not supported sound on NSFW content or certain embedded files, including RedGIFs embeds. Its fix: tap the post source or link on the Reddit post, and the clip plays on RedGIFs with sound.
