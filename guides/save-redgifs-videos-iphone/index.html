How to save RedGIFs videos on iPhone

RedGIFs has no download button for iPhone. You can still keep a clip on your phone, with Safari alone or with an app that saves from the link.

With Safari only

  • Open the RedGIFs page in Safari and let the video start playing.
  • Press and hold the video. If Safari shows Download Video, tap it. The file goes to the Downloads folder in the Files app.
  • To put it in Photos, open it in Files, tap Share, then Save Video.

This works on some pages and not on others, because RedGIFs often plays the video in a way Safari will not offer to download. The saved clip also lands in your camera roll or Files with everything else.

With Pillowfeed

  • Copy the RedGIFs link, open Pillowfeed and paste it. Pillowfeed finds the video and saves it.
  • Or open the page in Pillowfeed's built-in browser and tap Save while the video plays.
  • Or tap Share in Safari or any app and pick Pillowfeed.

Saved clips stay out of your camera roll. They play as a full-screen feed you swipe through, behind Face ID, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your media. With Pillowfeed Pro the whole collection can live in your own iCloud instead of on the phone.

Save only what you have the right to keep.
