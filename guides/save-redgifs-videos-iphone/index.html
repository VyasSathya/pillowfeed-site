How to save RedGIFs videos on iPhone

You can keep a RedGIFs clip on your phone, with Safari alone or with an app that saves from the link.

With Safari only

  • Open the RedGIFs page in Safari and let the video start playing.
  • Press and hold the video. If Safari offers a download option, tap it. A downloaded file goes to the Downloads folder in the Files app; if you do not see Downloads, tap iCloud Drive.
  • To put it in Photos, open it in Files, tap Share, and choose the save option.

A clip saved this way sits in Files, and in your photo library once you save it to Photos, with everything else.

With Pillowfeed

  • Copy the RedGIFs link, open Pillowfeed and paste it. Pillowfeed finds the video and saves it.
  • Or open the page in Pillowfeed's built-in browser and tap Save while the video plays.
  • Or tap Share in Safari or any app and pick Pillowfeed.

Saved clips stay out of your camera roll. They play as a full-screen feed you swipe through, behind Face ID, and the app switcher shows a cover instead of your media. With Pillowfeed Pro the whole collection can live in your own iCloud instead of on the phone.
