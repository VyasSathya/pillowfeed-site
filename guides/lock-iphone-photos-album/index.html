Can you lock one photo album on iPhone?

No. Apple's pages describe no lock for a single album. What Photos has is a Hidden album and a Recently Deleted album that are locked by default, and a lock on the whole Photos app.

The closest options

  • Move the photos to the Hidden album. Touch and hold a photo and tap Hide. The album opens with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.
  • Lock the whole Photos app. Touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID.
  • Use a separate app. It has its own lock, so one album's worth of photos can live there. Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

Why hiding is not the same as locking an album

Hidden items leave your library views, so your albums and the Photos widget stop showing them. But all hidden photos sit in one album, and the same Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode opens it.

Common questions

Is there a way to lock my photo album on iPhone?

Not one album. The Hidden album is locked by default, and you can lock the whole Photos app with Face ID or keep photos in a separate app with its own lock.

How do I lock the Photos folder on my iPhone?

Apple's pages describe no lock for albums or folders in Photos. Use the Hidden album for single items, or Require Face ID on the Photos app.

Can I put a lock on an album?

Apple's pages describe no lock for one album. Only the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums are locked by default, and you can lock the whole Photos app.
