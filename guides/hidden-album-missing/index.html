Hidden photos and videos missing on iPhone: what to check

When hidden items are gone, they are usually in one of four places. Go through them in this order.

1. The Hidden album is switched off

Open Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn on Show Hidden Album. Then open Photos, go to Utilities, and tap Hidden.

2. They were deleted

Open the Recently Deleted album in Utilities. Deleted items stay there for up to 30 days before they are removed for good. A hidden item that was deleted goes there too. Select it and tap Recover.

3. You are on a different Apple Account

With iCloud Photos on, your library follows your Apple Account. Open Settings, tap your name at the top, and check that it is the account you used when you hid the items.

4. iCloud Photos has not finished syncing

On a new or restored iPhone, the library can take time to come down. Keep the iPhone on Wi-Fi and plugged in, and check Photos again later.

If none of these find them

A device that never had iCloud Photos on only has what is stored on that device. If the items were on another phone that is lost or erased, they cannot come back from here unless that phone has an iCloud or computer backup.

Common questions

How do I get my hidden album back on iPhone?

Check Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn on Show Hidden Album. Then open Utilities and Hidden. If it is still empty, check Recently Deleted and make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account.

What happened to my hidden pictures on iPhone?

They are in the Hidden album, deleted into Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, or on an Apple Account or device that is not the one you are using now.

Why don't hidden photos show up?

Hidden photos are removed from the main library and other albums by design, and they only show in the Hidden album.
