Can you hide a video on iPhone? Yes, three ways

Yes. The right way depends on who you are hiding it from, because each method protects against something different.

1. Hide it in Photos (a casual scroll)

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves your library views and goes to the Hidden album, locked with Face ID or your passcode. Good against someone flipping through your camera roll. It stays in your library and in iCloud Photos.

2. Put it in a locked note (a different password)

Add the video to a note and lock the note. You can use your passcode or a separate Notes password. The note's title stays visible, and the original stays in Photos until you delete it.

3. Keep it in a separate app (out of the camera roll)

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. It uses the same Face ID or passcode as the Hidden album, so it hides the videos from your camera roll, not from someone who knows your passcode.

What someone with your passcode can open

The Hidden album and a separate app both open for a person who knows your iPhone passcode. A locked note does not if it uses its own Notes password, different from your passcode. Keep the passcode private.

Common questions

Can you hide a video on iPhone?

Yes. Use the Hidden album in Photos, put the video in a locked note, or keep it in a separate app with its own lock.

Is there a secret folder on iPhone?

The built-in secret place is the Hidden album in Photos. It is locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, but it is part of your library and not a separate folder.

How do I hide a video in my camera roll?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves the camera roll and goes to the Hidden album.
