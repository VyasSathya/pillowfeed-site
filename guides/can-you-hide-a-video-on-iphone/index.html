Can you hide a video on iPhone? Yes, three ways

Yes. The right way depends on who you are hiding it from, because each method protects against something different.

1. Hide it in Photos (a casual scroll)

Open the video, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves your library views and goes to the Hidden album, locked with Face ID or Touch ID. Good against someone flipping through your camera roll. It stays in your library and in iCloud Photos.

2. Lock the Photos app (a borrowed phone)

Touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID. The library and the Hidden album then both need Face ID or your passcode. Notes is not a way to do this: Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked.

3. Keep it in a separate app (out of the camera roll)

Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free. It uses the same Face ID or passcode as the Hidden album, so it hides the videos from your camera roll, not from someone who knows your passcode.

Nothing hides it from someone with your passcode

All three open for a person who knows your iPhone passcode. Keep the passcode private.

Common questions

Can you hide a video on iPhone?

Yes. Use the Hidden album in Photos, lock the Photos app with Face ID, or keep it in a separate app with its own lock.

Is there a secret folder on iPhone?

The built-in secret place is the Hidden album in Photos. It is locked by default on iOS 16 and later, but it is part of your library and not a separate folder.

How do I hide a video in my camera roll?

Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, and tap Hide. It leaves the camera roll and goes to the Hidden album.
