How to hide videos on iPhone in the Notes app

Yes, you can put a video in a note, and a locked note hides it behind your passcode or a Notes password. It works, with three catches: the note's title stays visible, the original stays in Photos, and a forgotten Notes password cannot be recovered.

Step by step

  • Open Notes and start a new note. Give it a title that says nothing about the video.
  • Tap the attach button, choose Photo or Video, and pick the video from Photos.
  • Open the note's menu and choose Lock. The first time, Notes asks you to use your iPhone passcode or to set a separate Notes password. Face ID or Touch ID can open it afterwards.
  • Tap the lock icon to lock the note now. From then on, the content stays hidden until you open it with Face ID, Touch ID or the password.

The three catches

  • The title stays visible. A locked note stays in your list. You cannot hide the note itself, so the title is what anyone sees.
  • The original is still in Photos. Notes keeps a copy. Delete the video from Photos and empty Recently Deleted, or it is just as visible as before.
  • Forget the Notes password and the note stays locked. Apple cannot recover it. If you use a separate password, write it somewhere safe.

When a note is the wrong tool

Notes suits a few short clips. It is a poor home for a collection: there is no album view, no way to play them one after another, and a video sits inside a page of text. Common questions

Can I put videos in Notes on iPhone?

Yes. In a note, use the attach button, choose Photo or Video, and pick the video. Notes keeps its own copy, so the original stays in Photos until you delete it.

Is there a way to hide a video on iPhone?

Yes: move it to the Hidden album, add it to a locked note, or keep it in a separate app with its own lock. Each one copies or moves the video in a different way.

Can I hide a note in the Notes app?

No. A locked note stays in your list and shows its title, but its content is hidden until you open it with Face ID, Touch ID or the password. Pick a title that does not describe the video.
