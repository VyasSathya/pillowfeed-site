Can you hide videos in the Notes app on iPhone?

No. You can add a video to a note, but Apple's own guide says notes with video, audio, PDF or file attachments cannot be locked.

What Notes can lock

Notes can lock a note with your iPhone passcode or a password only for Notes, and Face ID or Touch ID can open it. It works for notes on your iPhone or in iCloud without those attachments, but not for Quick Notes, notes with tags or shared notes. A locked note still shows its title in your list.

Why a note is not a hiding place for video

  • A note with a video attached cannot be locked, so anyone who can open Notes can play it.
  • If you delete the original from Photos, it goes to Recently Deleted and stays there for 30 days unless you delete it there too.

What works instead

  • The Hidden album. Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, tap Hide. It opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Lock the Photos app. Touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID.
  • A separate app. Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

All three can open for someone who knows your iPhone passcode. With Stolen Device Protection on, a locked app away from familiar places needs Face ID or Touch ID instead. Keep the passcode private.

Common questions

Can I put videos in Notes on iPhone?

Yes, you can add a video to a note. But Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked, so the video is not hidden.

Is there a way to hide a video on iPhone?

Yes: the Hidden album in Photos, requiring Face ID on the Photos app, or a separate app with its own lock.

Can I hide a note in the Notes app?

Apple's Notes guide describes locking a note, not hiding it: a locked note stays in your list with its title showing. And notes with video, audio, PDF or file attachments cannot be locked at all.
