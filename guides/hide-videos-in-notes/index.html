Can you hide videos in the Notes app on iPhone?

No. You can add a video to a note, but Apple's own guide says notes with video, audio, PDF or file attachments cannot be locked. A note that cannot be locked hides nothing.

What Notes can lock

Notes can lock a note with a password, your iPhone passcode, or Face ID. It works for notes without those attachments, such as plain text. A locked note still shows its title in your list.

Why a note is not a hiding place for video

  • A note with a video attached cannot be locked, so anyone who opens Notes can play it.
  • Adding a video to a note makes a copy. The original stays in Photos until you delete it and empty Recently Deleted.

What works instead

  • The Hidden album. Open the video in Photos, tap the three-dot menu, tap Hide. It opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
  • Lock the Photos app. On iOS 18 and later, touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID.
  • A separate app. Pillowfeed keeps videos in their own app, apart from your camera roll. It opens with Face ID or your iPhone passcode, locks again after 5 minutes away, and shows a cover in the app switcher. The first 200 items are free.

All three open for someone who knows your iPhone passcode. Keep it private.

Common questions

Can I put videos in Notes on iPhone?

Yes, you can add a video to a note. But Apple says a note with a video attached cannot be locked, so the video is not hidden.

Is there a way to hide a video on iPhone?

Yes: the Hidden album in Photos, requiring Face ID on the Photos app on iOS 18 and later, or a separate app with its own lock.

Can I hide a note in the Notes app?

No. A locked note stays in your list and shows its title. And notes with video, audio, PDF or file attachments cannot be locked at all.
