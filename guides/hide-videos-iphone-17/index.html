How to hide videos on iPhone 17 (iOS 26 and iOS 27)

Hiding a video on an iPhone 17 works the same way as on other iPhones: you send it to the Hidden album, and the album asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

The steps

  • Open Photos and find the video.
  • Touch and hold the video, or open it and tap the three-dot menu, then tap Hide. Confirm when asked.
  • To find it again, tap Collections, scroll to Utilities and tap Hidden, then tap View Album and use Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Check that the album is locked

On iOS 16 and later the Hidden album is locked by default, and Apple says you open it with Face ID or Touch ID. The lock is the Use Face ID setting in Settings, then Apps, then Photos. Apple says that if you turn it off, the Hidden album is unlocked by default. If you also want it out of the Utilities list, open Settings, then Apps, then Photos, and turn off Show Hidden Album.

What it does not do

  • The video is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it. If you use iCloud Photos, it is hidden on your other devices too.
  • Anyone who can pass Face ID or Touch ID on your phone, or who knows your passcode, can open the album.

Common questions

How do I hide videos on iPhone 17?

In Photos, touch and hold the video, or open it and tap the three-dot menu, then tap Hide and confirm. It moves to the Hidden album under Utilities, which opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Is there a way to hide videos on iPhone?

Yes: the Hidden album, a locked Photos app, or a separate app with its own lock. The Hidden album is the built-in one.

Does hiding a video delete it?

No. It is not deleted, but it does not appear in Library or other albums until you unhide it. If you use iCloud Photos, it is hidden on your other devices too.
