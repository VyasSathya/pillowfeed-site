A Keepsafe alternative for iPhone

Keepsafe is built as a locker for photos and documents, with its own cloud and apps on other platforms. Pillowfeed is built for watching what you keep: your saved videos as a swipe feed, with links and pages saved straight in, and the collection in your own iCloud.

What Keepsafe lists

From the App Store page of Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe, read November 2026.

  • PIN, fingerprint Touch ID and encryption on photos and videos
  • Custom albums, full-screen and slideshow viewing
  • Photo backup and sync across Keepsafe installs on any platform
  • Integrated camera and Safe Send for time-limited sharing
  • Premium: Album Lock, Private Cloud (up to 10,000 items), Space Saver, Trash Recovery, no ads

What Pillowfeed does

  • Plays your photos and videos as a full-screen feed you swipe up through, newest first or shuffled
  • Opens with Face ID or your passcode and locks again after 5 minutes away; the app switcher shows a cover
  • Brings media in from Photos, Files, the share sheet, Shortcuts, a pasted link, or its built-in browser (tap Save on the video playing)
  • Hearts and Super favorites, a filter for videos with sound, and albums that play as their own feed
  • Edits in place, from crop to cutting a section out, with the original kept
  • No account, no ads, no tracking; the developer runs no servers
  • Pro keeps the collection in your own private iCloud and removes the copies from the iPhone
  • Exports one item or everything to Files
  • Free for up to 200 items; Pro is a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase

Moving over

Get your files out of Keepsafe first, to Photos or Files where it offers that, then add the files to Pillowfeed from Photos or Files, check they are all there, and only then delete anything. Try the feed with your own files in your browser first if you like: pillowfeed.com/feed. Nothing is uploaded.
