A Hide it Pro alternative for iPhone

Hide it Pro is built around disguise: a fake crash screen and an escape code. Pillowfeed is built around watching what you saved as a swipe feed, with saving from links and pages, and Pro storage in your own iCloud.

What Hide it Pro lists

From the App Store page of Hide it Pro (Hide Photos Video), read November 2026.

  • Photos and videos behind a lock screen, with automatic locking on exit
  • Multiple albums with custom names and thumbnails; GIF support
  • Built-in picture viewer and video player
  • Disguise screen that looks like a crash, and an escape code that shows an empty app
  • USB export, unhide back to the gallery, and cloud backup with an optional paid storage upgrade

What Pillowfeed does

  • Plays your photos and videos as a full-screen feed you swipe up through, newest first or shuffled
  • Opens with Face ID or your passcode and locks again after 5 minutes away; the app switcher shows a cover
  • Brings media in from Photos, Files, the share sheet, Shortcuts, a pasted link, or its built-in browser (tap Save on the video playing)
  • Hearts and Super favorites, a filter for videos with sound, and albums that play as their own feed
  • Edits in place, from crop to cutting a section out, with the original kept
  • No account, no ads, no tracking; the developer runs no servers
  • Pro keeps the collection in your own private iCloud and removes the copies from the iPhone
  • Exports one item or everything to Files
  • Free for up to 200 items; Pro is a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase

Moving over

Get your files out of Hide it Pro first, to Photos or Files where it offers that, then add the files to Pillowfeed from Photos or Files, check they are all there, and only then delete anything. Try the feed with your own files in your browser first if you like: pillowfeed.com/feed. Nothing is uploaded.
