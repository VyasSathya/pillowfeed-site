A Private Photo Vault alternative for iPhone

Private Photo Vault leans on lock features (break-in reports, decoy password) and an encrypted cloud of its own. Pillowfeed leans on watching: a full-screen video feed, saving the video playing on a page or from a pasted link, and Pro storage in your own iCloud.

What Private Photo Vault lists

From the App Store page of Private Photo Vault (Pic Safe), read November 2026.

  • PIN and pattern lock on the app, password-protected albums
  • Break-in report with photo and GPS, decoy password, face-down lock
  • Albums inside the app
  • Import and export with the Photos app, plus wireless transfer
  • Image gallery and slideshow; video import and playback
  • Private web browser that downloads photos into the app
  • Encrypted Cloud Vault backup with its own password, and Space Saver

What Pillowfeed does

  • Plays your photos and videos as a full-screen feed you swipe up through, newest first or shuffled
  • Opens with Face ID or your passcode and locks again after 5 minutes away; the app switcher shows a cover
  • Brings media in from Photos, Files, the share sheet, Shortcuts, a pasted link, or its built-in browser (tap Save on the video playing)
  • Hearts and Super favorites, a filter for videos with sound, and albums that play as their own feed
  • Edits in place, from crop to cutting a section out, with the original kept
  • No account, no ads, no tracking; the developer runs no servers
  • Pro keeps the collection in your own private iCloud and removes the copies from the iPhone
  • Exports one item or everything to Files
  • Free for up to 200 items; Pro is a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase

Moving over

Get your files out of Private Photo Vault first, to Photos or Files where it offers that, then add the files to Pillowfeed from Photos or Files, check they are all there, and only then delete anything. Try the feed with your own files in your browser first if you like: pillowfeed.com/feed. Nothing is uploaded.
