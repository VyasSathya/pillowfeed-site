Your saved videos, as a feed only you can open

Pillowfeed plays the photos and videos you keep as a full-screen feed on your iPhone.

  • Swipe up through your clips, newest first or shuffled.
  • Opens with Face ID or your passcode. No account, no ads.
  • Save from a link or the built-in browser. Pro keeps it all in your own iCloud.

Want to see how a feed of your own clips feels first? Try it in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
